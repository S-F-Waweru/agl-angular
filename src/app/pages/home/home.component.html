About AGL

Fabrication with a fitter's precision.

Alternative Glasses Limited is a Kenyan aluminium and glass fabrication company, building the details that hold spaces together — frames, panels, partitions and railings.

We work directly with homeowners, contractors and offices across Kenya, taking a project from measurement and design through to fabrication and on-site installation. Every piece is cut, framed and fitted to the space it's built for — not off a shelf.

Our workshop pairs aluminium framing with processed glass and stainless steel, so a single team handles the whole job: shower enclosures for homes, partitioning and curtain walling for offices and commercial buildings, and stainless steel railings for staircases, balconies and walkways.

  • Custom fitEvery piece measured and built to the exact opening.
  • One workshopAluminium, glass and steel fabricated under one roof.
  • On-site fittingOur own installers handle delivery and fixing.
  • Built to lastMarine-grade hardware and treated finishes.

How It Works

From site visit to fitted glass.

Six steps that run as one continuous loop — the final review feeds straight back into the next consultation.

01

Consultation

We discuss the project, intended use, preferred style, materials, finishes and budget.

02

Site survey

We visit and take exact measurements of the opening or space.

03

Design & quote

You get a spec — glass type, finish, hardware — and a firm price.

04

Fabrication

Aluminium, glass and steel are cut and framed in our workshop.

05

Installation

Our own fitters deliver and install on site, and check the finish.

06

Final review

We review the completed work with you and confirm it meets the agreed requirements.
