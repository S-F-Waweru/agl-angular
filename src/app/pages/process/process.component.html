Our Process

From Measurement to Installation

Every AGL project is handled with a clear, practical process designed to reduce errors and deliver a reliable finish. The seven steps run as one continuous loop.

01

Consultation

We discuss the project, intended use, preferred style, materials, finishes and budget.

02

Site Measurement

Our team measures the openings and installation areas to confirm accurate dimensions.

03

Contract Signing

The agreed brief and budget are drawn into a works contract and signed by both parties before specification or ordering begins.

04

Design and Specification

We agree on the design, glass type, aluminium profile, hardware and finish before fabrication.

05

Fabrication

Components are cut, processed, framed and prepared in our workshop.

06

Delivery and Installation

Our installers deliver the products and complete the fitting and fixing on site.

07

Final Review

We review the completed work with the client and confirm that the installation meets the agreed requirements.

Seven steps
One continuous loop
