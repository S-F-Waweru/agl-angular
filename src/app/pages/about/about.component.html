About Alternative Glasses Limited

Alternative Glasses Limited (AGL) is a trusted aluminium and glass fabrication company in Kenya, providing quality architectural glass, aluminium and stainless-steel solutions for residential, commercial and office projects. Based in Nairobi, we serve homeowners, contractors, architects, property developers and businesses across Kenya.

We build the details that hold spaces together—including aluminium windows and doors, glass panels, office partitions, curtain walling, shower cubicles, glass balustrades and stainless-steel railings. Every product is custom-measured, fabricated and installed to suit the exact requirements of your space.

As an experienced glass and aluminium company in Nairobi, we manage the complete project process, from measurement and design through to fabrication, delivery and on-site installation. Each piece is cut, framed and fitted for its intended opening rather than supplied as a standard off-the-shelf product.

Our Approach

Our workshop combines aluminium framing, processed glass and stainless steel under one roof. This allows us to provide coordinated solutions for homes, offices and commercial buildings while maintaining control over quality, measurements, fabrication and installation.

Our Promise

We combine practical fabrication expertise with careful workmanship to create glass, aluminium and stainless-steel solutions that are functional, durable and visually refined. Our goal is simple: to make every space work better and look better.
